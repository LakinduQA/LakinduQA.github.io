# About me

## Summary

Lakindu is a Quality Engineer who approaches quality as an engineering responsibility, focusing on understanding how systems work, investigating failures beyond the surface, and continuously building practical engineering knowledge through hands-on work.

## My approach

I'm a Quality Engineer who likes understanding what happens beyond "it works" or "it doesn't."

I learn by building things, breaking them, investigating what happened, and documenting what I learn along the way.

For me, quality isn't something you check at the end. It's something you engineer into a product throughout its lifecycle.

I want to understand the user, the business rule, the system behind the feature, and what could realistically go wrong before deciding how quality should be approached.

That mindset has pushed me deeper into automation, APIs, databases, performance, security, CI/CD, system behaviour, and the engineering concepts behind the products I work with.

## Principles

- **Understand the why:** Don't just learn how something works. Understand why it exists and what problem it solves.
- **Investigate deeper:** "It doesn't work" is usually the beginning of the investigation, not the conclusion.
- **Engineer quality early:** Quality should be considered throughout the software lifecycle, not checked only at the end.
- **Use the right tool:** Start with the problem and risk, then decide what approach or tool makes sense.
- **Learn by building:** Practical projects help turn concepts into something I actually understand.
- **Share what I learn:** Writing forces me to understand things better and may help someone else along the way.
