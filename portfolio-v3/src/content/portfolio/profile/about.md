# About me

## Summary

Lakindu approaches quality as an engineering discipline rather than a final checkpoint, combining exploratory thinking, repeatable test design, automation, and clear evidence so teams can understand risk and release with confidence.

## My approach

I am an Information Technology for Business undergraduate who learns by building, breaking, investigating, and documenting real systems.

My work combines exploratory thinking, structured test design, automation, and clear evidence. I try to understand the user, business rule, and risk before choosing a tool.

## Principles

- **Understand the why:** useful tests begin with the user and the risk.
- **Make evidence useful:** results should help a team reproduce, diagnose, and decide.
- **Learn in public:** projects and writing turn investigations into reusable knowledge.
